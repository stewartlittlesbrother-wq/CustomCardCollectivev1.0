Deck lists

When on, each player picks one of their saved decks and hands it in. It's checked against the card pool and ban list, you can see every list, and that's the deck they play with. Anyone who hasn't submitted by the start is removed.

Draft settings

Each pack has 12 cards. Both players draft at the same time from the chosen collections, then build on a shared clock; anyone still building when it runs out gets their deck filled from their pulls.
