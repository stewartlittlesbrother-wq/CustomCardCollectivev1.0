How to Play

Cards
↻ Double-click any card to rest or unrest it
👁 Hover a card to preview it
🖱 Right-click board cards for a move menu
DON!!
+ Click a DON card to select it (blue glow), then click a character or leader to attach it
− Right-click a DON card to remove 1 from your count
Life Cards
👀 Hover the life area to fan out your cards
🖱 Right-click a life card to reveal it or send it somewhere
Hand
🖱 Right-click a hand card to send it to trash, deck, or life
Deck
🖱 Right-click your deck to view all cards inside
Tools
→ Draw Arrow: click button, then click 2 cards to draw a red line between them
✎ Add Note: click button, then click any card to add coloured text on it
↺ Restand All untaps every card and detaches all DON
